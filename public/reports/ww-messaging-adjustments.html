Messaging Adjustments  /  World Within

Make the two voices one

Six adjustments drawn straight from your own copy. The message is already good. It is split across a warm social voice and an institutional web voice, and worn thin by two templates. These pull it back together.

Based on 91 social + 84 web assets Rust = Instagram · Green = Website
The one line everything ladders to
"We fund and film community-owned businesses, so wealth stays with the people who build it."
Put this in the site hero and the social bio. Every page and every post should be a specific proof of this one sentence. Right now the clearest version of it is buried in 20 duplicate meta descriptions.

The adjustments

Each is a specific change, why it matters in the data, and a before and after in your real copy.

01

Bring the first person onto the website

Website

Social says "we / our" in 65% of posts; the site says it in 5%. A visitor who falls for a reel and clicks through hits copy that stops talking to them.

Now"World Within is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that deploys donated capital into cooperatives, mutual banks, and community land trusts, and produces documentary films about the communities it invests in."
Adjust to"We move donated capital into the businesses communities own themselves: cooperatives, mutual banks, community land trusts. Then we film the people building them. We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit."
02

Name the model on social, not just the show

Instagram

On social the mission collapses to "cooperative" and "change the world." Mutual banks, land trusts, and "keeps wealth local" appear 0 times. The reach is real; the reason-why never rides along.

Now"[hook]… Watch the full episode on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and YouTube. Link in bio. #howtochangetheworld #podcast"
Adjust to"[hook]… [Guest] runs a bank owned by its depositors, not shareholders, so the profit stays in town. That is exactly the kind of business we fund. Full story in bio."
03

Open with the question, the one device that already crosses over

Both

The question is the only move both channels share (23% of social, 21% of web). It is the natural bridge for turning a web explainer into a scroll-stopper, and for opening a page with a hook instead of a definition.

Now · page title"What Is a Cooperative Business?"
Adjust to · hook + opener"Who actually owns your grocery store? In a food co-op, it is the people who shop there."
04

Retire "link in bio" as the reflex close

Instagram

61 of 76 captions end the same way. The CTA rarely matches what the post was about, so the ask does no work. Swap the reflex for one specific next step per post.

NowEvery post ends: "… Link in bio."
Adjust to · match the ask to the post
  • "Watch the film, free, in bio."
  • "Meet the co-op we funded in [place]: [link]."
  • "Help fund the next one: [link]."
05

Promote the strong line, retire the duplicate meta

Website

Your clearest sentence is repeated verbatim across 20 pages as the meta description, so Google sees near-duplicates and the line gets worn out. Use it once, where it counts, and describe each page on its own terms.

NowThe same 501(c)(3) sentence sits in ~20 page descriptions, and most /project and /story pages carry no unique copy at all.
Adjust toMake it the hero line and the social bio, once. Give every page a one-line description of that page, so the story pages become findable instead of invisible.
06

Make one contrast your signature

Both

The ownership idea is phrased a dozen ways. The "owned by members, not outside investors" contrast is your sharpest and most portable line, and it already reads well on the site. Standardize it and use it everywhere.

Now"owned and governed by its members," "no outside shareholders," "owned by its depositors"… a different phrasing on every page.
Adjust to · one signature frame"Owned by the people who use it, not shareholders who don't."

The quick rule per surface

Instagram · reach

+Open with a question, then name a real community-owned business and what its model does.
+End with one specific ask tied to the post.
−Default to "link in bio."
−Let "the podcast" be the whole message. The show is the hook, not the point.

Website · conversion

+Speak in the first person, the way the feed does.
+Lead pages with the question, then answer it. Write a unique description per page.
−Reuse the one positioning sentence as boilerplate meta.
−Open with a dictionary definition.

If you change three things this month

  1. Put the one line in the site hero and the Instagram bio.
  2. Rewrite the homepage and top pages into the first person (adjustment 01).
  3. Start naming the model in every social caption, not just the podcast (adjustment 02).

These three close the widest gaps, the tonal cliff and the missing "why" on social, without a full rewrite.
