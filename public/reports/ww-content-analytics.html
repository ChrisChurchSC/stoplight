06

What moves engagement

Effect sizes: the ratio in average likes between content with and without a feature. Association, not proof of cause.

Celebrity / partner vs co-op explainer24.9×
Reel vs single image6.3×
Question hook vs none3.9×
Reel vs carousel2.0×
February was an 87× month. Average views per reel hit 78,056 vs a ~900 median in other months, driven by three partner/celebrity reels (Arian Moayed twice, Coral Vita). Removing those three returns February to baseline, so the lift is concentrated, not broad.

Samples are small (question-hook n=19; celebrity n=14), so treat effect sizes as directional. Reach and likes are correlated with, not caused by, these features; production quality and timing are uncontrolled.

07

Methodology & limitations

Every figure is computed from the ingested library and the brand system; nothing is estimated. The method is transparent and the limits are real:

  • Keyword / n-gram matching. Proof-point and CTA presence is detected by distinctive-word overlap, so a proof point paraphrased very differently than its label reads as "unused." This undercounts coverage; the true figure is a ceiling on what's shown.
  • Exact-name audience join. Audience alignment matches tag strings to defined-audience names literally, so "Impact Investors" vs a defined "HNW Donors" reads as zero overlap. The finding is really that the taxonomies aren't shared.
  • Website is meta-only. For pages, only the SEO title and description were ingested, not the full page, so their CTAs and onward links aren't visible here. Read website connection as a floor.
  • Small samples on effect sizes. Feature groups are small (n=14–19), so engagement ratios are directional and correlational, not causal.
