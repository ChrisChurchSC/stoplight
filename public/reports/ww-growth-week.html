Every week

The loop

1
Read SignalsLibrary › Signals
The ten-minute "what's working and what's off": what converts vs what just gets reach, the messaging patterns, and the coverage checks, are we using our proof, making the ask, targeting our ICP, connecting the funnel. Replaces the Monday spreadsheet stitched from six dashboards.
In World WithinSignals shows 19 of 53 proof points ever make it into the copy, 52% of posts dead-end, and the subscribe ask appears in 3% of assets.
2
Pick the leverCoverage gaps
The gaps come ranked, so you choose the highest-leverage fix instead of guessing. One clear move beats ten vague ones.
In World WithinThe family-office segment is targeted by zero content and the Fund is named in 3% of assets. The move picks itself: an ABM push to Impact Investors & Funders.
3
Plan the campaignCampaigns
A campaign scoped to a segment inherits its angle, proof, and CTA automatically. Lay the multi-channel touches on the canvas, grid, or calendar. The readiness check flags incoherence, missing proof, a dead end, no CTA, before you spend a dollar.
In World Within"Family Office ABM," tagged to the segment: LinkedIn → email → gift-officer note → roundtable, sequenced Jul 8–29. The check flags that the proof isn't attached yet.
4
Generate the creativeAsk Claude
Draft each touch grounded in the brand system and what's already worked, with per-segment or per-account variants. The output-constrained part of the job gets real leverage.
In World WithinClaude drafts the LinkedIn thesis ("philanthropy that recycles") and the email one-pager, leading with the ranked proof: self-replenishing cycle → real portfolio → Earthshot / TIME.
5
Traffic itCalendar › Review
Sequence on the calendar, move cards draft → review → approved, hand off to the channels. Ads and the ESP execute externally; ItsyBitsy is the plan, the creative, and the source of truth.
In World WithinFour touches scheduled; the 1:1 slot fans out a per-account version for each enriched funder from its Clay data.
6
Report and triageOverview › Reports
Overview shows what needs attention and what's due next across every campaign. Reports generate the dated, on-brand write-up for the QBR or the board in a click.
In World WithinThe Content Analytics and Messaging reports brief the team; Overview flags the next asset due and any card stuck in review.

And in the moment

Any screen, hit Cmd+K to Ask Claude for a quick read ("how is our LinkedIn messaging drifting?"), a rewrite, or a fresh report, without leaving the tool or opening a dashboard.

Why it fits a growth marketer: every other tool either shows performance or holds the plan. ItsyBitsy checks whether the execution matches the strategy, reads the actual copy, and then drafts the fix. That's the layer between "what we said we'd do" and "what we shipped", which is exactly where growth work happens.
